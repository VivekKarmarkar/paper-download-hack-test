{\Huge\bfseries Paper\,$\to$\,Bibliography OS}\\[2pt]
{\large\color{wgrey} The commands you actually run. Everything else is plumbing these call.}\\[10pt]

\begin{flow}{wgreen}{\,1\ \ \ I already have everything}
\textbf{When:} you have a folder (or tree) of papers — a finished literature review — and just want the bibliography.\\[4pt]
\textbf{Run:}\quad \cmd{/generate-bibliography <folder>}\\[4pt]
\textbf{Get:}\quad \texttt{bibliography.pdf} — one merged, deduplicated, clickable-DOI references PDF. One shot, runs in the background.\\[2pt]
{\small\color{wgrey}(This is the workflow that cleanly produced 86/87 correct references on the real 87-paper review.)}
\end{flow}

\vspace{8pt}

\begin{flow}{wblue}{\,2\ \ \ Talk in batches, then merge}
\textbf{When:} your past papers are stored on disk, and you want to talk through new papers with Claude in batches (analysis, discovery) before producing the bibliography.\\[4pt]
\textbf{Run, repeatedly through the session:}\quad \cmd{/identify-papers-ai-ur <the papers you name>}\\
{\small\color{wgrey}\hspace*{1em}Each call resolves only the papers you reference and accumulates them (the AI cumulative).}\\[4pt]
\textbf{Then, once:}\quad \cmd{/generate-bibliography-cumulative <folder>}\\[4pt]
\textbf{Get:}\quad \texttt{bibliography.pdf} — your on-disk folder \emph{plus} everything you discovered in the batches, merged + deduplicated.
\end{flow}

\vspace{8pt}

\begin{flow}{worange}{\,3\ \ \ Walk-and-talk a section (disk-aware writing)}
\textbf{When:} a bibliography already exists on disk, and you go on a walk voice-noting a draft over Telegram, citing papers as you go (some already on disk, some new).\\[4pt]
\textbf{Run:}\quad \cmd{/voice-writing-sample-all}\\
{\small\color{wgrey}\hspace*{1em}Cumulative sister: \texttt{/voice-writing-sample-all-cumulative} (draw references from the across-runs pool).}\\[4pt]
\textbf{Get:}\quad an emailed writing-sample PDF. Reuses papers already on disk, resolves only the new ones, and numbers references \texttt{[1],[2],[3]\ldots} in order of first citation.
\end{flow}

\vspace{12pt}

{\color{wgrey}\small
\textbf{Under the hood.}\ Each command above is a \emph{composite} — it fans out into $\sim$20 other skills you never invoke directly (the \texttt{identify-papers-*}, \texttt{verify}/\texttt{validate}, \texttt{*-bibliography}, and \texttt{merge-*} families). They are the engine; these four are the dashboard. If you forget the rest, that's fine --- you are meant to.\\[6pt]
\textbf{The four load-bearing commands:}\\[3pt]
\hspace*{1em}\texttt{identify-papers-ai-ur}\hfill\texttt{generate-bibliography}\hspace*{1em}\\[2pt]
\hspace*{1em}\texttt{generate-bibliography-cumulative}\hfill\texttt{voice-writing-sample-all}\hspace*{1em}
